Recommended

Works on every plan, including Free (which allows one custom connector).

  1. Open Customize, then Connectors, and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Paste the URL above as the connector URL.
  3. Choose No sign-in (no authentication). Awning needs no account.
  4. In a chat, open +, then Connectors, and switch Awning on.
  5. Ask for something. Try the prompt below.
