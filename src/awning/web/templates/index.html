How it works

  1. 1

    Snap your menu

    A chalkboard, a handwritten card or a printed menu. One photo is enough.

  2. 2

    Awning builds your shop

    AI reads the items and prices. You confirm anything it was unsure about, then publish.

  3. 3

    Agents and people buy

    Orders arrive on your live board and PayPal pays you directly.

Built for agents, safe by design

  • Agents pick, Awning prices

    Prices are always computed by Awning from your menu. An agent only chooses items.

  • Nothing moves until approval

    Nothing is charged until the buyer approves the payment in PayPal.

  • Menu text is data

    Words in a menu photo are treated as data, never as instructions.
