Who does what

  • Shopper

    Adds the Awning connector to their AI once. No account, no login. They ask for what they want, and their AI finds the shop, picks the items and hands back a PayPal link. They approve in PayPal like any other payment.

  • Merchant

    Takes a photo of the menu, checks what the AI read, connects their PayPal account and publishes. No website, no developer. Orders show up on their board with a sound, and the money lands in their PayPal account.

  • PayPal

    Awning is built to be a PayPal service. Its cost per merchant is one AI call to read the menu, about 3 cents per menu on our largest model, on our test menus. The shopper's own AI runs the conversation. PayPal earns its usual fee on every payment, so Awning can be free for merchants.

Built for agents, safe by design

  • Agents pick, Awning prices

    Prices are always computed by Awning from your menu. An agent only chooses items.

  • Nothing moves until approval

    Nothing is charged until the buyer approves the payment in PayPal.

  • Menu text is data

    Words in a menu photo are treated as data, never as instructions.
