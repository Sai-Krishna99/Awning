# Examples

Everything here is made up for the demo. No real business is involved.

- `marias-cart/menu.json`: Maria's Cart, a fictional espresso cart. The app seeds it on startup as the demo shop, and its payments go to a separate PayPal sandbox business account.
- `menus/`: menu images you can upload at `/start` to try the merchant flow without a menu of your own.
  - `slice-corner-pizza-synthetic.png`: a computer-generated menu, also used by the extraction eval.
  - `rosas-taco-stand-ai-generated.webp`: an AI-generated image (Gemini) of a handwritten menu for the fictional Rosa's Taco Stand, used in the demo video. It is not a real photo.
