# Awning: impact statistic

Verified 2026-11-08. Source: Clutch press release, 2025-08-21, "2025 Small Business Websites Survey".

- Finding: 83% of small businesses have a website (up from 64% in 2018), so 17% are still offline. Of those without a website, 28% have no online presence at all.
- Press release: https://clutch.co/press-releases/smb-websites-2025
- Syndicated copy: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250821848152/en
